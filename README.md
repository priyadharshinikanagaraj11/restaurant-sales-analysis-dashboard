# 🍔 Restaurant Sales Data Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing restaurant sales performance across 5 cities — tracking revenue, product performance, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

![Dashboard Preview](screenshot%201.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **restaurant sales transaction data** across Berlin, Lisbon, London, Madrid, and Paris to identify top-performing products, revenue trends, and customer payment/purchase behavior. Built with interactive slicers for **Payment Method**, **Purchase Type**, and **City**.

## 🎯 Objective
- Identify the most selling product category by quantity and revenue
- Analyze revenue distribution and total sales across cities
- Track month-wise revenue trend over the year
- Understand customer preference in payment method and purchase type (Drive-thru / In-store / Online)

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers)
- Data Visualization (Column, Pie, Area charts)

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights
| Visual | Insight |
|---|---|
| Most Selling Product (Column) | Burgers generated the highest revenue (~₹3,76,999) among all categories |
| Payment Method (Pie) | Credit Card is the most preferred (47%), followed by Cash (30%) and Gift Card (23%) |
| Total Revenue by City (Column) | Lisbon and London are the top-performing cities |
| Month Wise Revenue (Area) | Revenue trend shows consistent growth from Oct to Dec, reaching ~₹4,37,401 |
| Average of Revenue (Pie) | Madrid contributes the highest average revenue share (38%) |
